About Us

Shenzhen Qinboxing Electronics Co., Ltd. is a professional electronic components trading company based in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. We specialize in sourcing and supplying a wide range of electronic components including ICs, resistors, capacitors, connectors, LEDs, and power modules for domestic manufacturers and R&D teams. With a commitment to quality and competitive pricing, we serve customers across various industries including consumer electronics, telecommunications, automotive electronics, and industrial automation.
